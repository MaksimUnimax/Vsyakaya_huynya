# R1 — Competition landscape for new-business / new-point lead products

## Status

`COMPETITION = STRONG__DIRECT_ANALOG_FOUND`

Execution date: 2026-09-15.

The commercial gap around ERVK is materially narrower than initially assumed. Russia already has products that sell fresh companies and, more importantly, at least one direct service that monitors newly appearing physical business points across maps/catalogs/registries and filters false openings.

## 1. Direct competitor: Parsing.agency — «Новые компании на карте»

Public page:

`https://parsing.agency/new-businesses`

Current positioning:

- monitors new organizations and points on maps, catalogs and registries;
- identifies who opened, where and in what category;
- gives fresh records with contacts;
- delivery formats: file, feed, API or web interface;
- can operate weekly, daily or hourly;
- distinguishes a new company from a new branch/network point;
- adds registry enrichment on request;
- claims to filter relocations, renames and duplicates so that only real openings remain;
- combines Yandex Maps, Google Maps, 2GIS, catalogs and registries;
- public stated freshness: a new organization enters the delivery within 1–2 days after appearing in monitored sources under daily monitoring;
- public starting project price: from `$150`.

Fields advertised per opening include:

- name/category/activity description;
- address/coordinates/region;
- phone/email/site;
- date the point appeared in source;
- marker `new company` vs `new branch`;
- registry data on request.

### R1 implication

This is a near-direct substitute for the qualified-result product R1 would need after the raw ERVK signal failed.

Therefore R1 cannot win merely on:

- `new physical point`;
- `dedupe/relocation filtering`;
- `contacts`;
- `API/feed`;
- daily/hourly updates.

The only plausible structural advantage would be a **pre-map official signal**:

`qualified ERVK event -> true new point before it appears on Yandex/2GIS/Google/catalogs`.

This advantage is NOT yet proven.

If ERVK events become useful only after cross-checking against the same maps/catalogs and the true opening is already visible there, Parsing.agency makes the product gap weak.

## 2. New-entity lead competitor: Спрос.Радар — «Радар Новых»

Public page:

`https://sprosradar.ru/novye`

Current public pricing:

- 25 qualified new companies: `990 ₽`;
- 100 companies: `2 990 ₽`;
- recurring flow: `4 990 ₽/month`, about 50 recently registered businesses per week (~200/month).

The product uses recent company/IP registration plus a viability check and public contact enrichment. It markets limited re-use/exclusivity of leads and replacement of inactive records.

### R1 implication

This competitor does not solve the physical-point event problem as directly as Parsing.agency, but it sets a low price ceiling for generic `new business` leads.

Generic EGRUL/EGRIP new-entity lists therefore remain `KILL` as an R1 differentiation thesis.

## 3. Generic B2B contact competitor: DealRocket

Public pricing page:

`https://dealrocket.ru/pricing/`

Current public tiers:

- Starter: `3 900 ₽/month` for 500 contacts;
- Growth: `5 900 ₽/month` for 1,000 contacts;
- Business: `14 900 ₽/month` for 5,000 contacts plus one custom segment.

The service offers broad company/employee/contact segmentation including new companies, suppliers, retail, auto services, pharmacies, manufacturers and many other verticals.

### R1 implication

A raw organization/contact database has very low differentiation and high competitive pressure.

## 4. Competition conclusion

The competitive landscape creates a hard commercial gate:

R1 must prove at least one of the following:

1. **Earlier timing:** qualified ERVK signal arrives materially before map/catalog competitors.
2. **Different event class:** ERVK exposes an operational/legal transition with commercial intent that competitors do not classify.
3. **Much higher buyer relevance:** a narrow vertical signal linked to a concrete purchase moment, not generic new-company data.
4. **Lower acquisition cost with high precision:** true new points can be isolated from ERVK cheaply enough to beat multi-source map monitoring.

If none is proven, `KILL` is appropriate because a direct established competitor already sells the target result.
