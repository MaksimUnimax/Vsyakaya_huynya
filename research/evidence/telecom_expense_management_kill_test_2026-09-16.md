# Strategy B Kill Test — Telecom Expense Management for Russia

Дата: 2026-09-16

Итог:

`KILL__DIRECT_RUSSIAN_TEM_PRODUCT_ALREADY_EXISTS`

## 1. Исходная гипотеза

Российский Telecom Expense Management layer класса Tangoe / Calero:

`операторские счета + корпоративные SIM/services + usage + тарифы`
→ inventory
→ invoice audit
→ выявление неиспользуемых/дорогих линий
→ right-sizing тарифов
→ лимиты/контроль
→ measurable telecom savings.

Global demand зрелый: Tangoe работает в TEM более 20 лет, управляет крупным технологическим spend и обслуживает крупные enterprise accounts; Calero также продаёт telecom/mobile expense management с invoice audit, inventory и dispute/optimization workflows.

- https://www.tangoe.com/news/tangoe-reports-first-half-2026-momentum-driven-by-larger-enterprise-commitments-and-rising-demand-for-advisory-services/

## 2. Direct Russian competitor — Tarifer Corporate

Current product:

- https://tarifer.ru/b2b/corporate
- https://tarifer.ru/

Tarifer Corporate прямо закрывает core Russian mobile-TEM thesis.

Публично заявлены:

- загрузка электронных счетов/детализаций операторов;
- поддержка более 50 форматов;
- единая база корпоративных номеров/расходов;
- анализ расходов по каждому номеру;
- автоматический подбор оптимального тарифного плана и дополнительных услуг;
- поиск нецелевых расходов;
- отключение/выявление неиспользуемых номеров;
- корпоративные лимиты;
- уведомления;
- отчётность для IT/руководителя/бухгалтерии;
- integration of Tarifer data into other enterprise systems.

Tarifer currently states 400+ enterprises actively use the product.

Its published result ranges include:

- 15–21% reduction from tariff optimization;
- 5–10% from non-target/unused-line cleanup.

Current public customer examples show substantial savings, including cases around 14%, 20–30%, 34%, 36%, 45–60% depending on customer baseline.

These are vendor/customer-reported cases, not a universal benchmark, but they prove the Russian buyer/value thesis is already discovered and sold.

These native tools are not neutral multi-operator TEM, but Tarifer already supplies that neutral optimization layer.

## 4. Why fixed-network expansion does not rescue this candidate

A Tangoe-sized enterprise platform also manages fixed telecom circuits, network services and global invoices.

Narrowing the Russian opportunity to:

`leased lines / fixed Internet / SD-WAN / enterprise carrier invoices`

would create a much smaller enterprise-only ICP and require contract/inventory/order/dispute integrations across telecom providers.

That is a feature expansion beyond an already existing Russian TEM vendor, not evidence of a greenfield category.

No structural wedge was found that Tarifer/operator tools/enterprise ITSM or telecom consultants could not add.

## 5. Gates

`OWNER_VERIFIABILITY_GATE`: would pass — savings are visible in actual bills.

`GENERAL_AI_SUBSTITUTION_GATE`: would pass for continuous invoice/inventory optimization.

`DATA_TRUST_GATE`: manageable, because telecom bills are sensitive but ordinary enterprise operational data.

These do not overcome direct incumbent evidence.

## 6. Final

`KILL__DIRECT_RUSSIAN_TEM_PRODUCT_ALREADY_EXISTS`

Do not reopen as:

- Tangoe for Russia;
- corporate SIM spend optimization;
- multi-operator mobile bill analyzer;
- telecom tariff optimizer;
- corporate telecom cost dashboard;

without a genuinely new workflow beyond the current Tarifer/operator layer.
