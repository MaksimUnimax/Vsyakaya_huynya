## New authority findings

### 1. Public access is a legal requirement, not an optional UI feature

Current Rules approved by Government Decree No. 725 of 27.05.2025 state that publicly accessible information contained in the unified notification register must be available free of charge on the official public portal of the unified register of control types.

The same Rules require the competent authority to publish a widget/part of the official notification-register site on its own official website.

Primary source:
- https://government.ru/docs/all/159159/

Implication: the Rospotrebnadzor widget is an official public surface of the same register and is a legitimate recovery path, not a third-party substitute.

### 2. Current notifications are injected automatically into the unified register

The Rules state that notifications filed through Gosuslugi/regional portals are sent automatically to the unified register of control types. The date of filing is the day the applicant completes and signs the notification.

Rospotrebnadzor also describes accepted notifications as automatically placed in the Unified Register of Notifications based on ERVK.

Sources:
- https://government.ru/docs/all/159159/
- https://zpp.rospotrebnadzor.ru/news/federal/543553

### 3. Strong but not yet primary-source-confirmed same-day timing signal

The official Honest Mark community article dated 21.01.2026 says notification information is entered into ERVK automatically on the filing day, while the Gosuslugi cabinet may reflect it within a day.

Source:
- https://markirovka.ru/community/system-data/uvedomlenie-o-nachale-osushchestvleniya-predprinimatelskoy-deyatelnosti-2026

This materially weakens the old `10-day publication delay` concern for the post-2024/post-2025 ERVK architecture, but DOES NOT replace a live newest-record latency measurement. The old 10-day period belonged to the legacy website publication regime.

### 4. Official current service description says the procedure is about one working day

A 26.01.2026 regional Rospotrebnadzor instruction states the registration procedure takes one working day and points users to the ERVK public search for confirmation by INN/OGRN.

Source:
- https://26.rospotrebnadzor.ru/press-center/pr/13366/

This is procedural evidence, not a measured public-portal SLA.

## Critical new external-validation route: first cash receipt

Rospotrebnadzor states that since 01.03.2025 tax authorities may transmit:

- the address/location of registered cash-register equipment; and
- the date of the first cash receipt generated by that equipment.

Rospotrebnadzor compares those first-receipt facts with the notification register to identify businesses that started activity without the required notification.

Official source:
- https://zpp.rospotrebnadzor.ru/news/federal/543553

This means `first cash receipt date` is a high-quality operational marker of actual commercial start for retail/service points. The underlying Rospotrebnadzor first-receipt subregister is not known to be public.

Public inspection records sometimes reproduce this fact in their grounds. Example public records indexed in 2026 explicitly contain first-receipt dates and state that no corresponding notification existed. Those examples prove the mechanism is used operationally, but they are a highly selected enforcement sample and cannot be treated as representative market data.

Discovery examples:
- https://prokuratura.top/index.php?erpid=74260041000121296406
- https://prokuratura.top/index.php?erpid=74260041000121364956

## Secondary derived-data possibility

Public ERKNM / inspection-derived pages sometimes expose:

- notification number such as `У001/...`;
- notification date;
- INN/OGRN;
- activity/OKVED;
- object address;
- in some enforcement cases, first cash receipt date.

This creates a possible supplementary cross-check dataset:

`notification date -> first cash receipt date`

However:

- coverage is selected by control/enforcement activity;
- businesses without inspections are absent;
- missing-notification cases are overrepresented in some searches;
- it cannot replace the primary ERVK sample.

Use only as supporting evidence or for targeted case studies.
